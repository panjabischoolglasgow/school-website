Welcome to Google Classroom

Google Classroom is used to share homework, class resources, announcements and important information with pupils and parents. To join your child's class, you will need the class code from their teacher.

How to Join Your Child's Class

To keep our classes safe, Google Classroom codes are not shown on the website.

  1. Ask your child's class teacher for their Google Classroom code. Teachers share codes with parents directly (for example, through the class WhatsApp group).
  2. Go to classroom.google.com or open the Google Classroom app.
  3. Click + then Join class, and enter the code.

If you are not sure who your child's teacher is, please email panjabischoolglasgow@gmail.com.
